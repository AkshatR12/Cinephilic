Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How do I enter the cinema using my booking? +
Upon booking confirmation, you will receive a digital E-Ticket with a unique reference number and a scannable QR code. Simply show this QR code on your mobile device at the cinema entrance turnstiles.
Can I cancel my tickets and get a refund? +
Yes! Tickets can be cancelled up to 2 hours before showtime directly from your "My Bookings" profile page. A 100% refund is credited back to your original payment method within 24 hours.
What payment methods are supported? +
We support all major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), UPI Instant Payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and Netbanking across all top banks.
Are 3D glasses included in the ticket price? +
For all IMAX 3D and Standard 3D screenings, sanitized premium 3D glasses are provided at no extra charge upon hall entry and collected after the show.
How does the promo code work? +
You can use promo code CINE50 during checkout for an instant ₹50 discount on all movie bookings!
